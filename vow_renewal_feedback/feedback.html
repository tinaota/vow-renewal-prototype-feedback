Before you start

This is a low-fidelity prototype. It exists to test whether the structure, the order of information, and the RSVP steps are right — before any of it gets built. Nothing is functional yet.

Please ignore, for now

  • Forms, buttons, and links — they don't submit or go anywhere yet
  • Colors, fonts, spacing, and photos — these are placeholders
  • Anything that looks unfinished or grey

Please focus on

  • Whether the right things are here, in the right order
  • Whether the RSVP steps match how your guests actually behave
  • Anything factually wrong or that doesn't sound like you (see Section 3)
  • What's missing
Rate items 1–5 (1 = not working, 5 = exactly right) and add a comment. The comments matter more than the numbers. For anything tied to a specific screen, leave a pinned comment in Figma instead of writing it here.

“I don't know” and “I hadn't thought about that” are useful answers. Please don't skip a question to be polite.
